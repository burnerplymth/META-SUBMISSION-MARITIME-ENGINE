PORT TWIN GEOMETRY - LICENCE AND ATTRIBUTION
============================================

The vector files in this directory (*.geojson, profiles/*.json,
manifest.json) are DERIVED from OpenStreetMap data.

    (c) OpenStreetMap contributors

OpenStreetMap data is made available under the Open Database Licence
(ODbL) 1.0:  https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
Copyright and licence notice: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

What was done to the data (a "Derived Database" / "Produced Work" under
the ODbL): tiles were fetched from the OpenStreetMap API 0.6 /map call
on the retrieval date recorded in each file; objects were classified
into layers, clipped to a per-port rectangle, simplified with a fixed
Douglas-Peucker tolerance, coordinates quantised to 1e-5 degree, and
small objects dropped to keep each file under ~400 KB.  Profiles
(profiles/*.json) are MODELLED estimates computed from that geometry.

Obligations to carry forward when you redistribute these files (summary,
NOT legal advice - read the licence):
  * keep the attribution "(c) OpenStreetMap contributors" visible wherever
    the data or a map made from it is shown;
  * state that the data is available under the ODbL and link the licence;
  * a publicly distributed Derived Database must itself be offered under
    the ODbL (share-alike), and the changes to the data are to be
    identifiable;
  * a map image rendered from the data (a Produced Work) needs the
    attribution but is not itself share-alike.

The Sentinel-2 underlays in imagery/ are separate: Copernicus Sentinel
data, free and open under the Copernicus data terms; see
imagery/<place>.json for the scene id, date and attribution string.
